# ADR-002: Planner Deferred

Status: Accepted

## Context
Automated intent decomposition (a planner) would add a new component before the register, evidence and exception model is proven. It would also obscure who decided how work was split.

## Decision
Intent decomposition remains manual until Control Plane v1 is proven operational.

## Consequences
- Work items are created by people, not generated by a planner, in v1.
- A planner may be reconsidered only after Control Plane v1 is proven operational, through a new ADR.
