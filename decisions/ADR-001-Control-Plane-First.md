# ADR-001: Control Plane First

Status: Accepted

## Context
Control Plane v1 must prove that ownership, evidence, exceptions and derived status can be operated reliably. Planning, DCRR and optimisation depend on those foundations, and would hide their gaps if built first.

## Decision
Implement ownership, evidence, exceptions and derived status before planning, DCRR, or optimisation.

## Consequences
- Control Plane v1 is delivered and proven operational before any planner or optimisation work starts.
- Planning, DCRR and optimisation are out of scope for v1 streams.
